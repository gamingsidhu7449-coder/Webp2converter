guide

Understanding the WebP image format

01

What WebP actually is

WebP is an image format developed by Google in 2010 as an alternative to JPG, PNG, and GIF. It produces files that are significantly smaller than traditional formats without a noticeable drop in visual quality — which matters because image weight is often the biggest factor slowing a page down.

02

Lossy or lossless, your choice

Unlike PNG, which only supports lossless compression, WebP compresses either way depending on the content. Photos benefit from lossy WebP, while logos and icons benefit from lossless WebP — both come out smaller than the original format.

03

Works everywhere already

Browser support for WebP is now near-universal — Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari, and Opera all render it natively. There's little practical reason to avoid it for a modern website today.
