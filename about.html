about us

A small tool, built to do one thing well

Convert2WebP started from a simple frustration: most online image converters were slow, cluttered with ads before you could even find the upload button, or required creating an account just to convert a single file. We wanted something faster — a tool that opens, does the job, and gets out of your way.

What we do

Convert2WebP is a free, browser-based tool that converts JPG, JPEG, and PNG images into the WebP format. All conversion happens locally in your browser using standard web technology — your images are never uploaded to a remote server, which means faster conversions and better privacy for you.

Who it's for

Web developers optimizing page speed, bloggers preparing images for a post, online sellers preparing product photos, or anyone who simply needs a smaller image file without installing software. The tool is free to use, with no daily limits and no account required.

Our approach

We keep the site lightweight and focused on the converter itself. Where we add explanatory content, it's written to genuinely help you understand image formats and compression — not just to fill space. If you have feedback on how to make the tool more useful, we'd like to hear it.
